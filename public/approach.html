Working With MaxImpress.

A practical account of what happens from your first inquiry through planning, a demonstration period, secure account access, delivery and reporting.

From your first question
to a working plan.

01

Define what you need

Begin with your main objective, your existing social platforms and the results you want to work toward. This gives our team enough context to recommend a relevant service rather than an unrelated package.

Your goals, platforms and priorities
02

Contact our customer service team

Use the project form, email or a requested free strategy call to discuss your requirements. You can share account links, current challenges and available brand materials before a proposal is prepared.

A clear first conversation
03

Choose the service and scope

Review Creator, Organization, Brand or platform-specific support and select the services that suit the project. Deliverables, number of platforms, content requirements, time commitments and pricing are clarified in writing.

Defined services and expectations
04

Receive a plan of action

Before a trial begins, we organize the agreed project direction: content priorities, roles and approvals, proposed scheduling, campaign requirements and the way progress will be reviewed. Nothing goes live without the permissions agreed for the account.

Proposed plan, approvals and timeline
05

Explore a 4–6 day workflow trial

For eligible Creator, Organization and Brand Social Media Management engagements, MaxImpress may offer a 4–6 day free trial. It demonstrates the proposed working process, such as preparing draft content, scheduling examples, coordination and feedback checkpoints. It is not a promise of guaranteed follower or revenue growth in a few days.

A demonstration of the working rhythm, when offered
06

Approve secure account access and begin

With the scope agreed, the team discusses suitable platform-supported permissions. Clients retain account control; delegated, role-based or business access is preferred where available. Work proceeds only after the appropriate authorization.

Clear ownership and authorized access
07

Review work, results and continued service

Receive the agreed content, updates and reporting. Review what was delivered, approve changes where necessary and decide on renewal or the next phase. Payment timing and methods follow the written invoice and service agreement.

Performance review and next steps

Account safety

Your accounts.
Your control.

MaxImpress does not normally require your account password. Access is discussed using the permissions and delegation options supported by each platform.

X delegation

Where supported, an account owner can delegate appropriate access instead of handing over their login password. The available roles depend on X's current settings.

Meta business permissions

Facebook and Instagram business access can be organized through Meta's supported business and page permissions, with access limited to the agreed work.

YouTube channel permissions

Channel owners can grant role-based access using available channel permissions. Editing, publishing and reporting responsibilities are agreed before access is requested.

TikTok account access

Available business, organizational and team-access methods are reviewed for the account type. The exact permission model depends on the platform's current features.
