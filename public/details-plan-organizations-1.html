What this service covers

For organizations, professional teams and institutions, social media needs coordinated messaging, predictable approvals and consistent public communication. This service brings content planning, channel management, stakeholder-facing posts and reporting into one accountable workflow, while respecting the organization’s decision-making structure.

Service inclusions

  • Planning/formatting/scheduling/publishing
  • Relevant audience and professional network growth
  • Platform distribution and account management
  • Approval-led organizational communication
  • Reporting and improvements

Strategy & planning

Planning starts from the organization’s communication objectives, stakeholder groups, responsible approvers and sensitivities. Each channel is adapted to its intended audience, while the organization retains approval authority.

Stakeholder audiencesApproval authorityOfficial messagingContent calendarBrand governanceReport visibility

How this plan works

  1. Confirm organizational audiences, roles, approvals and any sensitive communications.
  2. Build a publishing plan and route posts through the organization’s authorized approvers.
  3. Publish after approval and share agreed activity/performance updates.
Before work begins We confirm channels, quantities where specified, required client inputs, approvals, timing, access permissions, revision arrangements and any exclusions in your written project scope. Platform-controlled results are not guaranteed.
