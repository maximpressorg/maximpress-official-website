## Security and integrity notes
- Front-end-only page gating is a navigation/UX limitation, not secure hiding of HTML source or static pricing. Restricted pricing needs private server-side storage and verified backend authorization.
- No historical likes/dislikes were invented; displayed totals are calculated from real verified-user votes in Firestore, initialized at zero.
- Ratings for old reviews are left unknown unless original verified rating evidence is supplied and inserted by an administrator.
- Existing user-supplied platform review badges are retained without increasing or embellishing endorsements.
- New customer feedback is pending until reviewed by an administrator; publication requires approval.
